## 1. Readiness verdict

**READY FOR CLINICAL REVIEW. Not a release candidate.**

The course's structure is sound:
- The trainer manual and all 314 slides match.
- All 20 blocks are 60 minutes.
- Case reveals are fully protected.
- Specialist procedures are held at the declared depth.

Seven blockers stand between it and a release candidate (section 4):
- two missing deliverables;
- five scientific and citation corrections;
- one conflict with a recorded approved decision;
- missing DRAFT and status markings on the decks.

## 6. What the audit confirmed as working

- **Reveal control.** Every fact about Reem and Tarek appears first in its scheduled block, and the participant manual contains no case data or answers. This is better protection than most course packs manage.
- **The trainer manual and decks are in lockstep.** All 314 slides match by number, type and title; all 44 run-sheet cues point to the right slides.
- **Depth discipline.** Specialist procedures stay at indications and limits. The resource demonstration is explicitly "بالكلام بس، من غير BLS"; parts work stays at the level of language. The Day 4 closing slide states which procedures need separate training.
- **Timing is fully reconciled**, from each task up to the course total.
- **Safety provisions:**
  - content warnings with a five-second pause before sensitive reveals;
  - a named wellbeing assistant with a breakout-room route;
  - "no personal disclosure required";
  - camera-off and break permission;
  - follow-up with affected participants after each day.
- **RTL production.** RTL and bidi formatting in the Word files is structurally sound. It has not been checked visually.
